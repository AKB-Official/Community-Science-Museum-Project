Our Upcoming Events

Harverd Proffesor Visit All Summer Long

This summer we will get the opportunity to meet and learn from renowned Harvard professors who will be visiting our museum for an extended period.

Science Fair

We are hosting a science fair where students and enthusiasts can showcase their innovative projects and experiments. Join us to witness the future of science!

Space Exploration Workshop

Join us for an interactive workshop on space exploration, where participants can learn about the latest advancements in space technology and even try their hand at building model rockets.

Robotics Competition

Witness the excitement of our annual robotics competition, where teams from various schools and organizations compete to showcase their robotic creations and problem-solving skills.

Environmental Awareness Campaign

Participate in our environmental awareness campaign, where we will host workshops, talks, and activities to educate the community about sustainable practices and the importance of protecting our planet.

Science Film Festival

Experience the wonders of science through our film festival, featuring documentaries and films that explore various scientific topics and discoveries. Join us for screenings and discussions with filmmakers and scientists.
